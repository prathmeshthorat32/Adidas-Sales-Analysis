# Adidas Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Adidas US sales data using Microsoft Power BI
to understand sales performance across regions, states, retailers,
and product categories.

The project includes an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to
help stakeholders monitor sales trends, compare regional performance,
analyze product demand, and support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Adidas aims to evaluate its sales performance across different
regions, states, retailers, and product categories to identify key
revenue drivers and profitability opportunities.

The objective is to provide a centralized dashboard for monitoring
sales trends, regional performance, product demand, and retailer
performance.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Units Sold
- Average Price per Unit
- Average Profit Margin (%)

These KPIs provide a quick overview of overall business performance.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes:

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by State
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Product
- Sales by Retailer
- Region Slicer
- Invoice Date Slicer

## 🔍 Analysis Areas

The project analyzes:

- Monthly sales trends
- Regional sales performance
- State-level sales distribution
- Product performance
- Retailer performance
- Profitability
- Units sold
- Average selling price
